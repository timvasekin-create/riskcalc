About RustDeck

RustDeck is a free toolkit for Hyperliquid traders. Paste any wallet address to see account value, realized and unrealized PnL, win rate, profit factor, open positions with liquidation distance, spot holdings and recent fills. The hub also streams a whale feed of $250K+ trades, live perp prices, funding rates, the official Hyperliquid leaderboard and a crypto ⇄ USD converter built on live mark prices.

Every wallet gets a shareable RustDeck Score (S–D) built from win rate, profit factor, average win/loss ratio and max drawdown. Alerts — wallet activity, position opens and closes, liquidations, limit orders and price targets — are delivered by the Telegram bot, while the position calculator turns entry, stop-loss, take-profit and leverage into exact size, margin, fees and liquidation price.

Is RustDeck free? Do I need an account?

Yes — the wallet tracker, markets, funding rates, converter and position calculator are free and work without sign-up. A Google account is only needed to link Telegram alerts and keep your saved wallets in your profile.

How do I track a Hyperliquid wallet?

Paste the 0x address into the tracker at the top of the page and hit Track Wallet. RustDeck reads public Hyperliquid data only — no keys, no wallet connection — and shows balance, PnL by period, win rate, positions, open orders and recent trades.

Where do the prices and 24h percentages come from?

Prices, 24h change, funding and open interest come straight from the Hyperliquid API (mark price versus the price 24 hours ago), so the numbers match the exchange UI. A backup source is used only if Hyperliquid is unreachable.

How do Telegram alerts work?

Sign in with Google, request a link code, then send /link CODE to @RustDeckcryptobot. Watch up to 5 wallets and get pings when a position opens, closes, gets liquidated or when a price target is hit. 7 days free.
